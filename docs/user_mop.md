# User Operations Manual

## 1. Purpose

This guide describes the standard hiring workflow for hiring managers and candidates using TalentOS. Available actions depend on role permissions and the services enabled in the deployment.

## 2. Hiring manager workflow

### 2.1 Sign in

1. Open the TalentOS web application.
2. Enter the registered email address.
3. Complete the one-time password verification.
4. Continue to the manager workspace.

### 2.2 Create a job

1. Open **Jobs** and choose **Create Job**.
2. Enter the job title, department, location, employment type, seniority, and role description.
3. Add the required skills and experience expectations.
4. Review the assessment stages and available scheduling window.
5. Save the job as a draft or publish it when it is ready.

Keep the role description accurate and avoid adding candidate personal data to the job description.

### 2.3 Review candidates

1. Open the relevant job.
2. Use the candidate import or resume synchronisation workflow configured for the organization.
3. Review the parsed candidate profile, matching score, strengths, gaps, and supporting evidence.
4. Open the original resume when additional context is required.
5. Update the candidate status according to the hiring process.

Matching scores are decision-support signals. Review the resume and role requirements before taking a hiring action.

### 2.4 Shortlist and invite

1. Select the candidates to progress.
2. Confirm the target role and shortlist selection.
3. Use the invitation action to send the candidate the next-step instructions.
4. Review the invitation status and resolve delivery issues before scheduling.

### 2.5 Manage interview schedules

1. Open the scheduling area for the relevant job.
2. Add or review available interview slots.
3. Confirm the interview window and time zone.
4. Share the booking invitation with shortlisted candidates.
5. Monitor booked, cancelled, and pending slots.

### 2.6 Review assessments

1. Open the candidate's assessment record.
2. Review section scores, written or transcribed answers, assessment notes, and integrity events.
3. Inspect any flagged event before making a decision.
4. Record the next action in the hiring workflow.

Assessment scores and integrity indicators should be reviewed alongside the full candidate record. They should not be treated as a sole basis for an employment decision.

## 3. Candidate workflow

### 3.1 Register and sign in

1. Open the candidate portal from the invitation or organization URL.
2. Register with the requested contact details.
3. Verify the email address using the one-time password.
4. Complete the face-registration step when required by the configured sign-up flow.
5. Sign in using the available verification steps.

### 3.2 Complete an assessment

1. Open the assessment invitation and confirm the job and scheduled time.
2. Review the device, camera, microphone, and network permissions.
3. Read the assessment instructions before starting.
4. Answer each prompt clearly and submit the response. Spoken responses may be transcribed when local speech capture is enabled.
5. Complete any requested presence or liveness challenges.
6. Submit the assessment and wait for the completion confirmation.

Do not refresh or close the assessment page while a response is being submitted. Contact the hiring team if the session is interrupted.

## 4. Operational support

For OTP delivery, camera, microphone, scheduling, or assessment-session issues, capture the approximate time, job reference, and a non-sensitive error message, then contact the organization's support team. Do not send passwords, OTP values, access tokens, or candidate documents through informal channels.
